Casino Session Tracker — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11/22/2026

Casino Session Tracker ("the app") is a personal tool for logging your own casino play — buy-ins, cash-outs, bonuses, comps, and related notes you choose to enter. This policy explains exactly what the app does with your data. The short version: your data is stored in your own Google Drive, and the app's developer never receives or stores it.

What the app stores, and where

Your tracker data stays in your Google Drive. Everything you enter is saved to a single file that the app creates in your Google Drive account. That file is the only place your data lives. There is no developer-run server or database that holds your information.

  • Sign-in information. The app uses Sign in with Google to identify you. From Google it receives your name, email address, and Google account identifier, solely to sign you in and to connect to your Drive. This is not stored on any server operated by the developer.
  • Your tracker content. The casino sessions, bankroll trips, comps, household names, game list, and settings you enter are written to the app's file in your Google Drive.

How the app uses Google services

The app requests the drive.file permission. This is a narrow, per-file permission: the app can only see and manage files that it has created or that you open with it. It cannot see, read, or touch any of your other Google Drive files.

The app also requests the standard openid, email, and profile sign-in scopes used by Sign in with Google.

Limited Use disclosure. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide and improve the app's user-facing features, is never sold, is never used for advertising, and is never transferred to others except as needed to provide the app or as required by law.

Sharing

The app does not sell your data, does not use it for advertising, and does not share it with any third party. Because your data lives in your own Google Drive, no one but you (and anyone you personally give access to your Google account) can reach it.

Analytics and tracking

The app does not include third-party advertising or cross-site tracking. Minor preferences (such as which tab you last viewed) may be kept in your browser's local storage on your own device.

Your control and deletion

  • Delete your data at any time by deleting the app's file from your Google Drive, or by using any delete controls inside the app.
  • Revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

The app is intended for adults of legal gambling age and is not directed to children.
